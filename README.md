# Mazda-Miata-Ground-Up-Re-Build
Rebuilt a bare-chassis 1999 Mazda Miata from the ground up (2024–2025). Re-Built the engine, drivetrain, brakes, and full interior, solving complex misfires and clutch issues along the way. Demonstrates hands-on mechanical aptitude, systematic problem-solving, and patience—turning a box of parts into a reliable daily driver.
## Technical Overview

Purchased a non-running, bare-chassis 1999 Mazda Miata delivered in parts and executed a complete mechanical overhaul and interior restoration. Transformed a box of unorganized components into a fully functional, reliable daily driver over a 10-month period.

## Key Work Performed

  - Engine & Drivetrain: Rebuilt engine, overhauled transmission shifter, and replaced power steering pump, spark plugs, and ignition coils.

  - Brakes & Hydraulics: Installed new calipers, rotors, pads, and a complete clutch assembly (clutch disc & pressure plate).

  - Interior & Cabin: Replaced dashboard, carpeting, and interior trim; restored electrical harnesses.

## Engineering & Diagnostic Competencies

  - Systematic Troubleshooting: Diagnosed and resolved complex engine misfires and clutch engagement issues using structured, root-cause methodology.

  - Blueprint & Torque Precision: Executed precise torque specifications across high-stress drivetrain and suspension components for operational safety.

  - Project Execution & Resilience: Managed end-to-end component mapping, assembly sequencing, and physical machine servicing without initial disassembly documentation.
